Integrated Technology Support

Laptop-ready tracks for digital careers.

For selected pathway programs, eligible learners can finish with both skills and the tech to use them.

What the program includes

In designated laptop-eligible tracks, qualifying learners may receive:

  • A current laptop prepared for training and job search
  • Core software for email, video calls, and documents
  • Guidance on safe use, updates, and storage
  • Tips for affordable internet and local access points

Why it matters

  • No more missed classes because a computer wasn’t available
  • Online applications and assessments can be done from home
  • Remote roles and hybrid jobs become realistic options
  • Learners can keep practicing and learning after graduation

Eligibility, device details, and distribution are set with each funding or referral partner. Laptop support is tied to full completion of specific pathway programs, not every course.

For learners
  • Clear expectations from day one
  • Help setting up the device and accounts
  • Support in using the laptop for interviews and onboarding
For agencies & workforce boards
  • Documented eligibility and distribution
  • Simple reporting to match your rules
  • Better completion and employment numbers
Remote Delivery

Built to work online and on paper.

We use consistent tools, clear structure, and tracked engagement so virtual cohorts stay strong and funder-friendly.

How our remote model works

  • Live sessions delivered through a stable, browser-based platform
  • Small groups with cameras-on expectations and check-ins
  • Attendance, progress, and assignments captured in one system
  • Recordings and slides shared for learners who need review

Accessibility & design choices

  • Low-bandwidth options and dial-in access when needed
  • Captioning tools and screen-friendly handouts
  • Simple links and calendars instead of complex portals
  • Device checks at the start of each cohort

The structure is built to line up with public workforce expectations: clear schedules, defined hours, and documentation that shows who attended, who completed, and what was covered.

For learners Remote classes run at predictable times with clear reminders, simple log-in steps, and easy ways to ask for help.
For partners You receive attendance, engagement notes, and completion data that support compliance, monitoring, and audits.
For employers Remote learning builds comfort with video calls, online tools, and digital communication — skills most modern workplaces expect.
